\begin{center}
  \textsc{Abstract}
\end{center}
%
\noindent
%
Bayesian calibration of gravity-driven mass flow models requires repeated exploration of a posterior distribution and may depend strongly on the selected sampling method. This report presents \texttt{mcmc-bench}, a modular framework that integrates Random-Walk Metropolis-Hastings, emcee, dynesty, PyMC Slice, and PyMC Sequential Monte Carlo through Nextflow and UM-Bridge. All methods calibrate the Voellmy friction parameters $\mu$ and $\xi$ using the same RobustGaSP surrogate, observations, priors, and likelihood. The samplers produced similar posterior means and standard deviations in the case study. In the single benchmark run, dynesty required the fewest surrogate-model evaluations and achieved the highest normalized efficiency. The results demonstrate that sampler-specific software environments can be integrated into a reusable workflow while maintaining a common statistical target and output format. The performance findings remain specific to the selected problem, configurations, and execution environment.
